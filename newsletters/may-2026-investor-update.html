What Broke & How We Fixed It

Issue

FSSAI State License — 3 weeks behind plan.

We budgeted 7–10 days. State license is actually 30–60 days. This is the gate for commercial Hyderabad sales — nothing moves without it.

Fix: Engaged Raag Consultants, Hyderabad (verified food business agent). ₹35K all-in. Cuts remaining timeline to ~12 days. Clearance expected June 18.

Issue

Blast chiller — ₹40K over capex estimate.

The unit we specced came in over budget. Blast chilling is a non-negotiable CCP — we move product from 60°C to 4°C in under 4 hours post-cook. No shortcut here.

Fix: Sourced refurbished unit from Shree Kitchen Equipments, Hyderabad — identical spec, 18-month warranty, ₹38K under new price. Decision confirmed June 3. Emergency reserve untouched.

Issue

Portion variance — 15% of Batch 3 above 150g target.

No food safety issue. But we're pricing for 150g. At commercial scale, uncontrolled portion weight quietly destroys the margin model. Caught this early — in the campus kitchen, not at 3,000 units/month.

Fix: Tightened intake SOP — every piece logged by weight at source. Supplier cuts to spec. Batches with >5% variance rejected. Batch 4 came in clean.
